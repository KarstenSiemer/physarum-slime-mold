## Slime, dead ends and the growth front (plan of 2026-11-16)

The owner asked for this on 2026-11-16 and agreed to the plan the same day. Aims: the colony does not grow back into dead ends that it left; the app shows the slime that the plasmodium leaves behind; the ends of growth look like the crown of a tree (the tube is the trunk, the ends split again and again and get finer outward, and the crown moves forward with the growth). The rule of the tubes stays as it is.

A fresh agent starts here: read [docs/knowledge-base/slime-mazes-and-the-front.md](docs/knowledge-base/slime-mazes-and-the-front.md) first. It has every fact below with its source, its page and how it was read, the equations of the models of a front, and what each stage must read; ADR 0052 records the plan and the decisions, ADR 0053 what stage 1 measured about the dead ends, and ADR 0054 the mark of starved ground that stage 1b built. The notes of 2026-11-16 (`papers/notes-2026-11-16-slime-and-front.md`) stay on the owner's machine as the raw record, untracked, with the PDFs beside them. One stage is one pull request with its ADR, both backends and its measurement. Stages 2 and 4 are mostly design: the owner runs them at the highest effort.

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-16:

- The crown is not only drawn. The growth front gets a role in the flow, so that plasm flows in the crown and a trunk forms from the rule of the tubes that the app has (stage 4).
- The front is a sink only while it grows, and only a birth onto fresh ground counts (ground without slime). So a front that reached the end of a dead end has no sink, its tube starves by the rule in force, and deaths and births in place keep no tube alive.
- One slime for all strains.

Read in the code on 2026-11-16, not measured:

- Flux exists only between food sites with a role (`docs/model.md`, 8.4 and 8.5). A tube that leads only to a growing front carries no flux, so no trunk can form toward a front.

Measured in stage 1 on 2026-11-16 (ADR 0053; the commands are part 17 of `research/flow/README.md`), with the mark of starved ground of that day:

- **The repair alone does not reach the first aim.** The rule is built: with the flow, no child is born onto a starved cell (D < 0.01), and the test of the cell of the parent stays. It stopped a third of the births in the dead ends of the Snail maze, and no measure changed.
- **The cause was the mark of starved ground.** A cell is closed for births while D < 0.01. D falls only while the cell holds a tube, and a starved tube is gone soon. So each cell stopped at a D of its own between 0.0001 and 0.01, about a fifth of the ground of a dead end was open at any time, and the colony came back as a wave: a blind branch filled about twice in 10,000 steps.
- **The rule that stage 2 plans for the slime does not keep the colony out of a dead end.** Measured with the mark of starved ground in the place of the slime (the variant `weight`: the weight 0.025 in the pick, no effect when every free neighbour has the mark, no mark on a cell with a tube body): the blind branches hold 19 % of the trail of the route (16 % without it). In a dead end that the colony left, every free neighbour has slime, so the weight never acts there. A wall for births in place of the weight (the variant `left`) changes nothing either: a move does not read the mark, so agents walk onto the ground, their trail gives cells a tube body, and children follow there (this reason is read from the rules, not measured apart).
- **The cell of the child alone is worse than both cells** (the variant `child`): the tube of the route breaks at times. Keep the test of the cell of the parent when stage 2 changes the pick.

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-16, after the result of stage 1: the repair stays, and the cause gets more analysis and a fix in a pull request of its own (stage 1b), so that the first aim is reached.

Built and measured in stage 1b on 2026-11-17 (ADR 0054; the commands are part 18 of `research/flow/README.md`). Read this before the design of stages 2 and 4:

- **The mark is the same in every cell now, and it lasts.** In the step in which a starved tube vanishes, its ground falls to the floor of the flow memory, 0.0001. Ground without a tube recovers by 0.002875 % per step. So starved ground is open for births again after 160,182 steps (3.7 days of model time; 22 minutes at the default speed of 120 steps per second) and fresh again after 6 days. The 6 days are the longest avoidance of slime that a source reports, second hand. The time is the constant `FLOW.recover`; there is no setting.
- **The first aim is reached for the life of the mark.** Snail maze, 10 seeds: the blind branches hold 0.4 % of the trail of the route (17.6 % before), all 60 are below 10 %, and no child is born in the dead ends from step 20,000 on (917 to 5,670 per 1,000 steps before). Corn maze: 89 to 91 % of the tube cells lie on the shortest route (60 to 74 %). The Maze does not change.
- **When the mark has faded, the colony comes back once.** In runs to step 220,000 the blind branches of the Snail maze fill between the steps 173,000 and 183,000, each for 1,000 to 3,000 steps, and they are empty and closed again after it. Every mark that fades gives such a return: with the 18 h of before it came 20,025 steps after the ground closed. Only the time is a choice.
- **The mark stops births, not moves.** Agents walk off a tube onto closed ground and starve there with 1 to 2 % per step. They draw short twigs of 20 to 60 cells beside the tube, and 13 to 77 of them die per 1,000 steps in the dead ends of the Snail maze. Of the cells with a tube off the route, 98 to 100 % lie on closed ground (`GROUND=1` of `measure-mazes.ts`). In the Corn maze these twigs cross every door of a side route in about a fifth of the dumps, which the measure counts as a tube on that route.
- **A tube that vanishes before it starved leaves no mark** (D from 0.01 on), and a child can be born there: 3 to 6 % of the ground of the dead ends of the Snail maze, and 8 to 10 % in the Corn maze. The variant `mark` of `variant.ts` closes this ground too. It was rejected: the Tokyo scene then needs up to 13,000 steps to join four cities whose food came back (2,000 with the rule of the app).
- **The Tokyo scenes run a flow strength of 2.8** (1.4 before). The long mark makes the network leaner: with 1.4 the total length is 1.45 ± 0.14 at step 48,000, and with 2.8 it is 1.72 ± 0.06 (the organism: 1.75 ± 0.30 at 26 h, which are 46,800 steps).
